1. What this is

The Google OAuth app named "Home Assistant" (the "app") connects one household's own Google Nest devices to that household's self-hosted Home Assistant server. It is a private, non-commercial setup.

2. Who may use it

Use is limited to the owner of the connected Nest devices and members of that household. The app is not offered to the public. No one else is authorized to sign in to it.

3. No fees

The app is free and has no paid features.

6. Ending use
