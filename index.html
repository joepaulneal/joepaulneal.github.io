Home Assistant – Neal Household

A private, non-commercial home automation setup.

"Home Assistant" is the name of a Google OAuth app used by one household to connect its own Google Nest devices (a doorbell and thermostats) to its own, self-hosted Home Assistant server.

What it does

  • Shows the status of the household's Nest thermostats and lets the household change their settings from Home Assistant.
  • Shows the Nest doorbell's live view and doorbell, motion, person and sound events, including event snapshots.
  • Lets Home Assistant receive device events from the household's own Google Cloud Pub/Sub topic.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Nest devices and members of that household. It is not offered to the public, is not sold, and has no other users.
